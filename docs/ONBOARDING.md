# Onboarding: every question, in order

CarePaws is launching in **Hyderabad**, and every account is real. These are the screens a person sees, what they are asked, and what is checked. All of it is enforced by the security rules, not only by the forms.

## Before onboarding: sign-in

Phone number (OTP), Google, or email and password. "Just looking around" can browse without an account.

## Screen 1: How will you use CarePaws? (everyone)

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| I have a pet | Parent setup |
| I'm a pet sitter | Sitter setup, with ID verification |
| Just looking around | Skips setup. Browse sitters, rescues and vets. Set up later from the You tab |
| I run a rescue or shelter / I'm a vet or clinic (shown under a link) | Listing setup |

Note shown: *CarePaws is launching in Hyderabad first. Elsewhere? You can still look around.*

## Pet parent (3 steps)

**Step 1. Verify your mobile number** (skipped if they signed in with their phone)
- Mobile number (Indian, 10 digits, starts 6–9), then the 6-digit SMS code.
- One number per account. Never shown on the profile.

**Step 2. A little about you**
1. Name (required)
2. Your neighbourhood in Hyderabad (required, chosen from a list of 67 areas; never an address)
3. About your household (optional, up to 600 characters)
4. "I'm 18 or older, and I agree to the Terms, Pet care policy and Privacy notice" (required)

**Step 3. Who are we caring for?** (can be skipped, but booking needs a pet)
- Kind of pet: Dog, Cat, Bird, Rabbit, Small pet, Reptile
- Name (required), Breed, Age
- Care sheet, which sitters read before accepting and which is copied into every booking:
  - Vaccinations: Up to date / Partly vaccinated / Not vaccinated, and the date of the last one
  - Sex, Weight (kg), Neutered or spayed, Microchipped
  - Behaviour (tick all that apply): Friendly with dogs, Friendly with cats, Good with children, Shy or anxious, Separation anxiety, Reactive on the leash, Afraid of loud noises, Escape artist, Guards food or toys, Has bitten someone
  - Medical conditions and allergies, Medication, Food and routine
  - Your vet (name) and the vet's phone
  - Anything else

**Asked later, at the first booking** (not in onboarding): care location, dates, a 10-point declaration list (vaccinations up to date, parasite prevention, health and behaviour fully disclosed, no contagious illness, owner authorised, conditions recorded at drop-off, inherent risk, emergency treatment authorisation with a spending limit, and the policy), 3 more if care is at the parent's home (pet-proofed home, hazards and cameras disclosed, safe key sharing), and an emergency contact.

## Pet sitter (4 steps, then admin review)

**Step 1. Verify your mobile number** (same as above)

**Step 2. A little about you**
1. Your photo (required: a clear photo of your face)
2. Name (required)
3. Your neighbourhood in Hyderabad (required)
4. About you and your home (required, at least 30 characters)
5. "I'm 18 or older, and I agree to the Terms, Pet care policy and Privacy notice" (required)

**Step 3. Your sitter profile**
- Services you offer (at least one): Overnight stays, House sitting, Drop-in visits, Dog walking, Medication, Special needs care, Pet taxi, Grooming, Training reinforcement
- Pets you can care for (at least one): Dogs, Cats, Birds, Rabbits, Small pets, Reptiles
- Days you're available (all seven by default)
- Your home: Apartment / House / Farm or large plot; Secure fenced yard; Smoke-free; Children at home; I have pets of my own (and which); Longest a pet is ever left alone (never alone, or up to 2, 4, 6 or 8 hours)
- Nightly rate in rupees (₹100 to ₹1,00,000)
- Experience, and usual reply time (within an hour, a few hours, or a day)

**Step 4. Verify your identity** (private, reviewed by CarePaws in 1–2 working days)
1. Full legal name, as on the ID
2. Date of birth (must be 18 or older)
3. ID type: Aadhaar (masked), Passport, Driving licence, Voter ID (EPIC), PAN card
4. Last 4 characters of the ID number. The full number is never collected
5. Photos: ID front, ID back (where the ID has one), and a selfie
6. Home address, with the city fixed to Hyderabad and a Hyderabad pincode (500, 501 or 502)
7. An address proof, only if the ID is a PAN card
8. Emergency contact: name and phone
9. Police clearance certificate (optional; earns a "Background checked" badge)
10. Care standards. Every one must be ticked:
    - Everything I've told CarePaws about myself, my home and my ID is true, and the documents are mine.
    - My home is safe for animals: windows and balconies are secured, fences and gates are escape-proof, and toxic plants, chemicals and medicines are out of reach.
    - Any pets I live with are vaccinated, have no history of aggression, and are listed on my profile.
    - I won't leave a pet alone for longer than the maximum hours on my profile, and never in a car or locked outdoors.
    - I'll send the pet parent at least one photo update every day of a stay.
    - I'll care for the pet myself and won't hand them to anyone else or sub-let the stay.
    - I'll use only kind, humane handling: no hitting, shock or prong collars, or confinement as punishment.
    - If a pet seems unwell or is injured, I'll contact the pet parent and a vet straight away, and log it in the app.
    - I'll report any injury, escape, fight or other incident to the pet parent immediately and record it in the stay log.
11. Consent to CarePaws processing the ID, selfie and address, with the right to withdraw and to deletion

The sitter is listed and bookable only after an admin approves the case.

## Rescue or shelter / vet clinic (listing)

1. Organisation name, neighbourhood in Hyderabad, About, and the 18+ and terms tick
2. Public phone number (this one is published on purpose), street address
3. Pin your location (refused if outside Hyderabad)
4. Vets only: Open 24×7 for emergencies. Everyone else: opening hours
5. What you offer or how you help, and the animals you help
6. Website (optional)

The listing appears only after an admin verifies it.

## What is enforced, not just asked

- A parent or sitter can't finish onboarding without a verified mobile number or a Hyderabad neighbourhood.
- A sitter can't go live without a submitted identity case, a rate and at least one pet type.
- Sitter pincodes must be Hyderabad ones, and a listing's pin must lie inside Hyderabad.
- Closing the app part-way resumes at the right step, and nothing already saved is asked again.
